## Pictures, films and sound

- Everything in `public/images`, `public/photos`, `public/films` and the photographs inside `public/projects` was generated with an image model for this template. They are placeholders: replace them with your own work.
- The "client site" screens in `public/projects` are mock-ups of invented brands, drawn for this template.
- `public/audio/click.mp3` and `whoosh.mp3` were synthesised for this template. The playground music is generated live in the browser by `src/lib/synth.ts`.
